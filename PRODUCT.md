# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static HTML, CSS and progressive JavaScript, as requested. No framework or build required to use the finished site.

## Users
A New Zealand secondary school art teacher and their NCEA Painting students at Levels 1, 2 and 3.

## Product Purpose
A classroom resource library with a page for each level and a separate printable task sheet for each of 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4, 3.2, 3.3 and 3.4.

## Operating Context
Students make physical paintings and drawing studies. Teachers adapt timing and assessment conditions. NZQA is the primary authority; tasks must distinguish suggested learning activities from official assessment requirements.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Current Level 1 Visual Arts standards with painting tasks (confirmed). Painting-specific Level 2 and 3 standards. Student-facing printable task sheets with teacher notes. Links to NZ and international artists; reproduce examples only where permitted. Source dates and standard IDs must be visible. Contemporary Māori, other New Zealand and international artist models should lead the artist connections, as requested in the revision.

## Brand Commitments
Colourful art-room workbook (confirmed). Name is an implementation choice, not a school identity. No invented NZQA endorsement.

## Evidence on Hand
Official NZQA achievement standards, assessment specifications, exemplar and moderator pages researched during creation. Artist and image sources must be recorded in the site.

## Product Principles
- Each task sheet addresses one standard.
- Make grade differences actionable through visual evidence.
- Keep student choices and cultural context central.
- Print well and work without a network except for external resources.
